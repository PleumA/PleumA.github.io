03 / ABOUT

From medicine to machines.

My background is in medicine, but my long-term direction is increasingly technical. I'm interested in understanding how software, mathematics, statistics, and artificial intelligence can be used to solve difficult problems in healthcare.

Rather than treating AI as a tool that sits on top of healthcare, I want to understand the engineering and computational foundations underneath it — from algorithms and data structures to machine learning systems.

My goal is to become an AI engineer and researcher who can move fluently between clinical problems and technical solutions.
